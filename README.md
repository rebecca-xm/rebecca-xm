<h1 align="center">Hi, I'm Rebecca 👋</h1>

<p align="center">Software developer. I studied Latin and Greek before I studied code.</p>

My work splits into two kinds of projects.

The first is research and development, mostly in healthcare. I've built platforms
that follow patients during clinical studies, assessment tools for clinicians, apps
that collect data from wearables, and an RFID tracking system. These projects
usually start from a clinical protocol or a spec that's still half an idea. My job
is to turn that into something that runs on real data, for people who make
decisions based on what it shows them. That's a good reason to get the details right.

The second is management software for industrial companies: orders, warehouse
stock, credit limits, purchasing workflows. It has to stay in sync with the
company's ERP and with how the company actually works, which isn't always what the
spec says. Most of the logic there lives in SQL Server, and I spend a lot of time
inside stored procedures.

On both sides, a lot of the work ends up on a phone. I build cross-platform mobile
apps with Ionic and Capacitor, from push notifications and native integrations to
getting the app through store review, which can be a project in itself.

Day to day that means .NET and C# on the backend, plenty of T-SQL, and Angular on
the frontend (plus some AngularJS that's still around). A fair share of the job is connecting to
third-party APIs and finding out why they don't behave the way their docs say.

What I write at work stays in private repos. What you'll find here is
[Pinax](https://github.com/rebecca-xm/pinax), a project board I'm building in my
own time, because I got tired of jumping from one GitHub Projects board to another.
It's also where I can try out architecture choices without a client deadline.

If any of this sounds interesting, or you just want to talk shop, you can find me
on LinkedIn.
